# APL Apollo Tubes (APLAPOLLO.NS): News Report as of 25 Nov 2026

## What happened

- **Tata Steel deal (the main driver).** Tata Steel announced plans to acquire a 26.38% stake in APL Apollo for about Rs 15,511 crore and to launch a mandatory open offer for another 26% [4]. A headline in the same item calls it a Rs 28,000-crore deal [4]. The items do not reconcile these figures, so the total consideration is unconfirmed. The open offer price and terms are not reported in these items.
- **Share price reaction.** APL Apollo shares fell about 13% on 23 Nov, with Tata Steel down 5% [4]. Market reports that day show APL Apollo and Tata Steel slipping while metals led a broad sell-off; Nifty Metal fell over 2% [3]. On 25 Nov, shares were down about 15% at 9:29 am, described as another fall after the earlier drop [5].
- **Group-linked stocks.** Related companies sold off harder on 25 Nov: SG Mart fell 19.44% and SG Finserve fell 17.46% [5]. This suggests the selling is spreading across the group, not only into APL Apollo.
- **Watch-list mentions.** APL Apollo appears in "stocks to watch" lists [1][2], but these carry no company-specific detail beyond the Tata Steel angle.

The Tata Steel stake purchase and mandatory open offer are highly material. They imply a change in control or a major new strategic shareholder, and they bring a mandatory tender that could set a price floor. The items do not say whether the open offer price is above or below current trading levels, so the implications for minority holders cannot be judged from this news alone.
