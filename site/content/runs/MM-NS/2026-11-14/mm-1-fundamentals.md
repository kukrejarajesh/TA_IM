# M&M.NS: Fundamentals Report (analysis date 2026-11-14)

**Net read: NEUTRAL, leaning positive.** Earnings are compounding well and free cash flow turned positive in FY26. Against that, debt is rising faster than equity, and interest costs are climbing quickly.

- **Revenue:** Growth accelerated to 24.8% in FY26 from 14.5% in FY25. Revenue reached Rs 198,639 crore.
- **Earnings:** Net income rose 32.3% to Rs 18,622 crore. Diluted EPS rose about 32% to Rs 137.50, so the share count appears broadly stable. Net income has grown well ahead of revenue since FY22.
- **Margins:** Operating margin eased to 18.6% from 19.2% in FY25, so FY26 growth came with some margin dilution at the operating level. Net margin improved because other income (Rs 5,118 crore, about 20% of pretax income) and operating leverage helped. Note that other income contributes a meaningful share of pretax profit.
- **Reconciliation:** Operating income plus other income, less interest and D&A, gives pretax income of Rs 25,185 crore. This ties to the table.

- **Leverage:** Debt/equity improved to 1.47x from 1.67x, because retained earnings grew faster than debt. Retained earnings rose Rs 16,057 crore in FY26.
- **Recent debt build:** Total debt rose from Rs 136,936 crore at March 2026 to Rs 154,094 crore at September 2026, an increase of Rs 17,158 crore in six months. - **Interest burden:** Interest expense rose from Rs 5,018 crore (FY22) to Rs 9,591 crore (FY26). Interest cover (operating income / interest) is 3.86x, up from 3.36x, so coverage is adequate and improving.
- **Liquidity and assets:** Investments and advances were Rs 40,324 crore at March 2026. "Other assets" make up most of the balance sheet (Rs 229,531 crore) and are not broken down, which limits how much I can say about asset quality.

- **Operating cash flow** recovered strongly to Rs 11,657 crore in FY26. It was negative in FY23 and FY24, and FY26 is the strongest year in the table.
- **Cash conversion:** Operating cash flow was 31.5% of operating income in FY26. Using the table's pre-tax proxy, cash flow conversion is about 0.3x, which is weak in absolute terms.
- **Free cash flow:** FCF turned positive in FY26 at Rs 2,545 crore. The gap between operating cash flow and FCF (Rs 9,112 crore) implies roughly that much capital spending. Over FY22 to FY26, cumulative FCF is negative at Rs -29,304 crore, so the growth has been funded externally.
- **Funding:** Financing inflows were Rs 5,391 crore in FY26 and Rs 15,834 crore in FY25. Debt has been the main funding source for the investment cycle.
- **Net cash flow** is close to zero each year, which is consistent with cash being used as fast as it comes in.

## 4. Data gaps and anomalies

- **Interim data:** The September 2026 balance sheet is present, but there is no matching income statement or cash flow, so the latest half-year trend cannot be checked.
- **Total assets equal total liabilities** in every period. This suggests the "Total Liabilities" row includes equity, so I have treated it as liabilities plus equity.
- **Missing lines:** There is no dividend, share count, tax, or detailed debt-maturity data. The tax rate and payout ratio were omitted from the source, so I have not computed them.
- **EPS check:** Net income of Rs 18,622 crore divided by diluted EPS of Rs 137.50 implies roughly 135 million shares. Common stock of Rs 559 crore would imply a different share count if the face value is Rs 5. This may reflect the diluted share basis, the consolidation scope, or a data issue. Verify before using per-share figures in a valuation.
- **Equity proxy:** My equity figure (common stock plus retained earnings) excludes any other reserves, so leverage ratios may be overstated.

## 5. Net read: NEUTRAL, leaning positive

**Positives**
- Revenue growth accelerated to 24.8%, and net income grew 32.3%.
- Operating cash flow recovered to Rs 11,657 crore, and FCF turned positive at Rs 2,545 crore after three negative years.
- Debt/equity improved to 1.47x and interest cover rose to 3.86x.

**Negatives**
- Operating margin fell to 18.6% from 19.2%.
- Debt rose by Rs 17,158 crore in the six months to September 2026.
- Interest expense has nearly doubled since FY22, and cumulative FCF over five years is negative.
- Other income is a material share of pretax profit.

**What would change the view**
- **Turn bullish:** Sustained operating margin recovery above 19%, FCF staying positive into FY27 as capex moderates, and debt growth slowing to below equity growth in the next full-year statement.
- **Turn bearish:** Debt continuing to rise faster than retained earnings, interest cover falling below about 3x, operating cash flow failing to keep pace with net income again, or a break in the EPS-to-share-count relationship once verified.
