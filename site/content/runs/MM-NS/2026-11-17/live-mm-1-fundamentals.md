## 4. Data gaps and oddities

- **Share count does not reconcile.** Common stock is Rs 559 crore. If the face value is Rs 5 per share, that implies about 112 crore shares, which would imply an EPS near Rs 166 on FY26 net income of Rs 18,622 crore. The reported Rs 137.50 EPS implies roughly 135 crore shares. The common stock line may not be the simple share capital, so per-share figures should be checked against the filings before use.
- **Balance sheet totals equal each other by construction.** Total liabilities equal total assets in every period, including 30 Sep 2026. Use the line items rather than the totals for analysis.
- **"Other assets" is a large residual.** It is Rs 229,531 crore at FY26, about 73% of total assets. Without a breakdown it is hard to judge asset quality.
- **No income statement or cash flow for 30 Sep 2026.** The latest half-year is visible only on the balance sheet, so H1 FY27 earnings and cash flow are unknown.
- **Omitted rows** (sales growth, margins, tax rate, payout, cash conversion) were rebuilt from the table as described above.

## 5. Net read: **NEUTRAL**

**Positives:** Revenue and earnings growth are strong and accelerating. Free cash flow turned positive in FY26. Retained earnings are building steadily.

**Negatives:** Operating margin is slipping. Debt grew 12.5% in six months, with leverage around 1.5x on an approximate basis. Free cash flow was negative for three of the last four years, and the FY26 improvement rests on one year. Part of the FY26 net-margin gain came from other income.

**What would change the call:**
- **Turn bullish if:** Operating margin stabilises or rises in FY27 and free cash flow stays positive for a second year without debt growth outpacing earnings. Debt flat or falling against retained earnings would be the clearest sign.
- **Turn bearish if:** Debt keeps rising faster than earnings, interest cost keeps growing ahead of operating income, operating cash flow falls back toward the FY25 level, or operating margin drops further.
- **Before acting:** Confirm the share count and equity figures, get the H1 FY27 income and cash flow statements, and check how much of the debt sits in financing subsidiaries. A share price is also needed to judge valuation.
