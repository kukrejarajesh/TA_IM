## 5. Net read: **Bearish (moderate)**

The business is still growing revenue, and it has earned strongly in good years (FY23 and FY25). The current picture is weaker:
- Operating margin fell to 14.6% and net income fell 21.8%.
- Operating cash flow fell to Rs 206 crore, and free cash flow was Rs -1,363 crore.
- Debt rose to Rs 5,670 crore, and the leverage proxy rose to 0.83x.
- Depreciation and interest are rising faster than cash earnings from the new capacity.

**What would move the view to neutral or bullish:**
- CWIP (Rs 3,640 crore at 2026-09-30) converting into commissioned capacity, with operating margin recovering toward the 18–19% range seen in FY23 and FY25.
- Operating cash flow returning to around net income or better, which would show FY26 was a timing or working-capital effect.
- Debt flat or falling from Rs 5,476 crore, and an explanation for the rise in other assets and other liabilities.
- H1 FY27 results that explain the Rs 737 crore retained-earnings increase and show operating income growing again.

**What would make it more bearish:** operating margin staying near 15% in the next annual report, negative free cash flow continuing, or debt rising above the current level without a matching rise in earnings.
