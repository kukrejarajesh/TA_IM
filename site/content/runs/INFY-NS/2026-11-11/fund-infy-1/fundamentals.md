# Fundamentals report: Infosys (INFY.NS)

**Analysis date:** 2026-11-11. **Latest annual period:** FY ending 2026-03-31. Interim balance-sheet rows dated 2026-09-30 are covered separately.

**Note on method:** The calculation budget ran out partway through, so some figures below are percentages worked out by hand from table values. No new rupee amounts are quoted beyond table values. Where a rupee difference would have been needed, I describe the direction instead of giving a number. Two corrections from my earlier draft: FY23 revenue growth is computable (FY22 is in the table), and FY24 cash conversion is computable.

## 1. Growth and profitability

- **Growth is slowing from FY23's spike, then picking up again.** Revenue rose 20.7% in FY23, then 4.7% in FY24, 6.1% in FY25 and 9.6% in FY26. The FY26 rate is the highest since FY23.
- **Earnings grew faster than revenue in FY26.** Net income rose 10.2%. Diluted EPS rose about 12.9%, which implies the share count fell by about 2%. The common stock line fell from Rs 2,073 crore to Rs 2,024 crore, consistent with buybacks, though a par-value change is also possible.
- **Operating margin has compressed.** It fell from 25.9% in FY22 to 23.7% in FY26, with a small rise in FY25 that reversed. Revenue growth is coming with thinner margins.
- **Other income is falling.** It dropped from Rs 4,711 crore in FY24 to Rs 3,033 crore in FY26, about a 36% fall. Other income is about 7.6% of FY26 pretax income of Rs 39,995 crore, so the earnings base depends more on operations than it did.
- **The income statement reconciles.** Operating income of Rs 42,280 crore, plus other income of Rs 3,033 crore, less interest of Rs 416 crore and D&A of Rs 4,902 crore, gives the Rs 39,995 crore pretax figure in the table.

## 2. Balance-sheet strength

- **Debt is low.** Total debt was Rs 9,176 crore at FY26 year-end, against total assets of Rs 154,288 crore. That is about 5.9% of assets. Debt has stayed between Rs 8,227 crore and Rs 9,176 crore over the five years shown.
- **Interest cover is very high.** Pretax income plus interest of Rs 416 crore is about 97 times that interest expense.
- **Other liabilities jumped.** They rose about 19.5%, from Rs 43,750 crore in FY25 to Rs 52,260 crore in FY26. Total liabilities rose about 4.4% over the same period. The data does not explain the driver, so it needs checking against the filing.
- **Retained earnings fell despite a profit year.** They went from Rs 93,745 crore to Rs 90,828 crore while net income was Rs 29,474 crore. Distributions and other charges therefore exceeded the year's earnings. The table does not split these into dividends, buybacks or other items.
- **Cash is not reported.** There is no cash line, so net debt cannot be computed. Net cash flow was negative Rs 2,254 crore in FY26 after a positive Rs 9,669 crore in FY25.
- **Equity is not reported as its own line.** Debt-to-equity therefore cannot be stated precisely from the table.
- **Interim balance sheet (2026-09-30).** Debt was Rs 8,525 crore, retained earnings were Rs 97,051 crore (up about 6.9% from March) and total assets were Rs 163,480 crore (up about 6.0%). This is supportive but unaudited and has no matching interim income or cash flow.
- **Data oddity.** "Total Liabilities" equals "Total Assets" in every period. This is the usual balance-sheet identity, which includes equity. The label is misleading, but the numbers are not wrong.

## 3. Cash generation

| Metric | FY26 | FY25 | FY24 | FY23 | FY22 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Operating cash flow (Rs crore) | 33,986 | 35,694 | 25,210 | 22,467 | 23,885 |
| Free cash flow (Rs crore) | 31,259 | 33,457 | 23,009 | 19,888 | 21,724 |
| FCF / net income | 106% | 125% | 88% | 82% | 98% |
| CFO / operating income | 80% | 91% | 69% | 64% | 76% |

- **Cash generation is strong in absolute terms.** FCF was Rs 31,259 crore in FY26 and covered net income (106%). FCF has been at least 82% of net income in every year shown.
- **Cash conversion dipped in FY26.** CFO was 80% of operating income, down from 91% in FY25. The FY24 and FY23 figures were weaker still, so FY25 looks like the outlier on the high side, not FY26 on the low side. The series is noisy.
- **CFO-to-FCF gap looks small.** The gap between CFO and FCF in FY26 is small compared with D&A of Rs 4,902 crore. Net PPE rose from Rs 30,961 crore to Rs 33,770 crore. Those table values suggest low net capex, or a definition of FCF that excludes some outflows such as leases. This needs checking.
- **Investing cash flow was positive in FY26.** It was an inflow of Rs 3,546 crore, against outflows in each of FY22 to FY25. This probably reflects investment sales or maturities, but the table does not show which.
- **Shareholder payouts are large.** Financing outflow was Rs 39,786 crore in FY26, up from Rs 24,161 crore in FY25, roughly a 65% rise. Payouts exceeded FY26 free cash flow, which explains the negative net cash flow. The dividend and buyback split is not in the table.

## 4. Data gaps and oddities

- No cash, equity, dividend, or capex rows. Net debt, payout ratios and capex intensity cannot be read directly.
- Other liabilities rose about 19.5% in FY26 without explanation.
- Implied capex from the table looks low against D&A and PPE growth.
- Interim (2026-09-30) balance-sheet data sits alongside annual data without interim income or cash flow.
- Total liabilities and total assets are the same figure in every period, so that label is not informative.

## 5. Net read: **Neutral**

**Supporting:**
- Revenue growth has picked up to 9.6% in FY26, the fastest since FY23.
- EPS growth of about 12.9% is helped by a lower share count.
- Debt is low (about 5.9% of assets) and interest cover is very high.
- FCF of Rs 31,259 crore covers net income (106%).

**Against:**
- Operating margin has fallen from 25.9% to 23.7% over four years.
- Cash conversion to CFO fell to 80% of operating income in FY26.
- Other income is down about 36% from FY24.
- Other liabilities rose about 19.5% without explanation.
- Shareholder outflows exceeded FY26 free cash flow, and retained earnings fell.

**What would change the view:**
- **More bullish:** operating margin holding above 24% while revenue growth stays near or above 10%; a filing-based explanation of the other liabilities increase showing it is operating in nature; cash conversion back near 90% of operating income; clear disclosure of cash and equity so net cash can be confirmed.
- **More bearish:** another margin decline; FCF falling below net income for a second year; a further rise in other liabilities or total liabilities relative to revenue; shareholder payouts continuing to exceed free cash flow, which would drain retained earnings further.
