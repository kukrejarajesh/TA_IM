5. **"Rs 963.20 is a defined downside."** That is a price level, not a thesis check. It would only trigger after a roughly 15% fall, and it says nothing about whether deal bookings or margins are deteriorating.

Keep Rs 963.20 as a hard review level, and add the sell trigger for margin below about 23%. Do not add on the bounce.
