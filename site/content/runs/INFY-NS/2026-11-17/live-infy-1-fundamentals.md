# Infosys (INFY.NS): Fundamentals Report (corrected)

**Analysis date:** 17 Nov 2026 | **Basis:** annual statements FY22 to FY26 (year ends 31 Mar), plus a 30 Sep 2026 balance sheet

**Calculation note:** The calculator's 30-call limit ran out partway through my work. Growth rates for revenue, and the FY26 vs FY22 revenue change, are tool-verified. Other percentages and ratios below are my hand calculations from table values, rounded to one decimal. I have not quoted any derived rupee amount that the tool did not return. Where a figure would need one, I quote the table rows instead.

## 1. Growth and profitability

- **Revenue** (table values): Rs 178,650 crore in FY26, Rs 162,990 crore in FY25, Rs 153,670 crore in FY24, Rs 146,767 crore in FY23, Rs 121,641 crore in FY22.
- **Revenue growth by year** (tool-verified): FY23 +20.7%, FY24 +4.7%, FY25 +6.1%, FY26 +9.6%. FY26 is +46.9% above FY22. The FY23 jump was the largest; growth then slowed and has picked up again over the last two years.
- **Net income** (table values): Rs 29,474 crore in FY26 against Rs 26,750 crore in FY25, up about 10.2%. From Rs 22,146 crore in FY22, that is about 33% over four years.
- **Diluted EPS:** Rs 72.59 in FY26 against Rs 64.32 in FY25, up about 12.9%. EPS grew faster than net income, which points to a lower share count. Common stock fell from Rs 2,073 crore (FY25) to Rs 2,024 crore (FY26), consistent with buybacks, though the par-value line is only a rough proxy.
- **Operating margin** (operating income ÷ revenue): about 23.7% in FY26, 24.1% in FY25 and 25.9% in FY22. Margin compressed from FY22 and has been broadly flat at around 24% since.
- **Net margin:** about 16.5% in FY26 against 18.2% in FY22.
- **Other income** fell from Rs 3,600 crore to Rs 3,033 crore (about -15.7%), after Rs 4,711 crore in FY24. Operations now make up a larger share of pretax profit.
- **Pretax income** was Rs 39,995 crore in FY26 against Rs 29,474 crore net income. The tax line is not reported, so the tax rate is not stated.
- **Depreciation and amortisation** rose to Rs 4,902 crore from Rs 4,812 crore.

## 2. Balance-sheet strength

- **Debt is low.** Total debt was Rs 9,176 crore at 31 Mar 2026, up from Rs 8,227 crore a year earlier, and Rs 8,525 crore at 30 Sep 2026.
- **Interest cover is very high.** Operating income of Rs 42,280 crore against interest expense of Rs 416 crore is roughly 100x.
- **Leverage:** total debt is roughly a tenth of common stock plus retained earnings (about 9.9%, hand-calculated). That equity proxy leaves out other reserves, so treat the figure as an approximate upper bound.
- **Retained earnings fell** from Rs 93,745 crore (FY25) to Rs 90,828 crore (FY26), even though net income was Rs 29,474 crore. The gap implies large distributions, but the source does not report them, so I have not quantified them. The 30 Sep 2026 figure is Rs 97,051 crore.
- **Asset base:** net PPE rose from Rs 30,961 crore to Rs 33,770 crore (about +9.1%). Investments and advances were Rs 21,880 crore at March 2026.
- **Other liabilities** rose from Rs 43,750 crore (FY25) to Rs 52,260 crore (FY26). This is the largest liability movement in the year, and the source does not explain it. It should be checked against the filing before any view is taken on it.

**Gaps and oddities:**
- No cash and cash-equivalents line is in the data, so net cash cannot be computed.
- Total liabilities equal total assets in every period (Rs 154,288 crore at March 2026). This suggests the liabilities line includes equity, so leverage ratios built on it are unreliable. I have not used it.
- Net income and net income including non-controlling interests are identical in every year, so no minority interest is visible.
- The 30 Sep 2026 balance sheet is a half-year point and does not say whether it is consolidated.

## 3. Cash generation

- **Operating cash flow:** Rs 33,986 crore in FY26 against Rs 35,694 crore in FY25 (about -4.8%). Earlier years were Rs 25,210 crore (FY24), Rs 22,467 crore (FY23) and Rs 23,885 crore (FY22).
- **Free cash flow:** Rs 31,259 crore in FY26 against Rs 33,457 crore in FY25 (about -6.6%). FY24 was Rs 23,009 crore and FY23 Rs 19,888 crore, so FCF has recovered strongly over two years but dipped in FY26.
- **Conversion:** CFO is about 1.15x net income and FCF about 1.06x, so reported earnings are well backed by cash.
- **CFO to operating profit:** the reference ratio needs CFO before tax, which is not in the data. Using CFO over operating income as a proxy gives about 80%. This is an approximation.
- **Cash flow in FY26:** operations generated Rs 33,986 crore, investing was a net inflow of Rs 3,546 crore, and financing was an outflow of Rs 39,786 crore. Net cash flow was -Rs 2,254 crore, after +Rs 9,669 crore in FY25.
- **Capital returns** exceeded FCF in FY26 (Rs 39,786 crore financing outflow against Rs 31,259 crore FCF), which is why cash fell. The source does not split the financing outflow into dividends, buybacks and debt repayment.

## 4. Net read: **Neutral**

**Positives:** revenue growth has picked up to about 10%, net income and EPS are growing, FCF covers net income, debt is small, and interest cover is very high.

**Negatives:** operating margin has been flat at about 24% and is below FY22. FCF fell about 6.6% in FY26, other income is shrinking, other liabilities jumped without explanation, and FY26 financing outflows exceeded FCF.

**What would change the view:**
- **Turn bullish** if operating margin holds above about 24% for a second year and FCF gets back above the Rs 33,457 crore of FY25. It would also help if the other-liabilities increase is explained as benign and the 30 Sep 2026 balance sheet is confirmed as consolidated.
- **Turn bearish** if revenue growth falls back below about 5%, operating margin drops below about 23%, FCF keeps falling, or the other-liabilities rise turns out to be a contingent or working-capital problem.
- **Data to obtain before acting:** cash and short-term investments, a reconciled equity figure, the tax charge (to compute CFO before tax and the tax rate), the breakdown of the FY26 financing outflow, a note on the other-liabilities increase, and the full-year FY27 results.
