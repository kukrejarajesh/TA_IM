## 5. Net read: **Neutral**

**Positive:**
- Revenue growth has been above 34% in each of the last three years.
- The business has moved from losses to operating profit, and the operating margin rose from 1.9% to 7.5% in FY26.
- Debt is low (4.9% of equity) and interest is covered about 13 times by operating income.

**Negative:**
- About half of pretax income (52.6% in FY26) comes from other income, not operations.
- Operating cash flow is small against operating income, and free cash flow is still negative.
- The balance sheet is hard to read: total liabilities equal total assets, there is no cash line, and retained earnings rose faster than net income in FY26.

**What would change the view:**
- **To bullish:** operating cash flow that stays positive and rises well above Rs 41 crore, free cash flow turning positive for a full year, and operating income growing faster than other income. A clear cash balance and a corrected balance sheet would also help.
- **To bearish:** revenue growth falling well below 30%, operating income falling back toward zero, further rises in debt, or negative operating cash flow. A fall in other income would also show how thin operating profit is.
